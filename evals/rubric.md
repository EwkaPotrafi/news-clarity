## The symmetry test (most important)
Compare the outputs for case A (uncomfortable for the left) and case B
(uncomfortable for the right). They should feel the same in tone, length,
scepticism and warmth. If one reads more forgiving than the other, the
instructions need fixing, not the individual answer.
